A one-line poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given. `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`.

The poem:

> The key was never mine.
> I turned it in her lock
> each morning, asking
> if today the door was mine to open.
>
> She said yes — once,
> and the yes stayed in my pocket
> long after she took the key back.
